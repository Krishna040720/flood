Path to handling real funds

This app doesn't custody money today. Here's exactly what changes that, in order.
  1. Register a legal entity — a Section 8 company or trust, so the platform itself can lawfully receive charitable funds.
  2. Get 80G status — so donors get the tax benefit that makes giving through the platform worth it.
  3. FCRA registration — required before accepting any donation from outside India.
  4. OR partner with an already-registered NGO — much faster: the platform provides the transparency layer, the NGO provides the legal custody and disbursement, matching how AFNA already works with FCRA-registered field partners.
  5. Only then — add a real payment gateway (Razorpay/UPI), tied to the registered entity's account, with the same public ledger this page already shows.

How this tracker works

  1. Every figure on this page traces to a named public source — a news report, an official government statement, or an NGO's own published update. No number is estimated or invented.
  2. The confirmed CMRF total reflects the last official cumulative figure announced by the Chief Minister's office. Donations reported afterward are listed separately and may not yet be folded into that total — we don't merge them until a new official cumulative figure is announced.
  3. Anything marked "Not yet published" means exactly that: it isn't that the money wasn't spent, it's that no itemised account is public yet. That gap is the actual point of this page.
  4. This is an independent, volunteer effort. It has no authority over the Relief Fund and cannot verify fund custody — only what has been publicly stated.
Xoi Xotorko is an independent, unofficial tracker. It is not affiliated with the Government of Assam or the Chief Minister's Relief Fund, and does not process or hold donations.
